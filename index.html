One Thing I Actually Wanted to Say

Okay, enough of the dramatic website stuff.

I actually wanted to say something properly.

It's still kind of funny to think that this whole friendship started with one completely random Instagram follow and a "Who are you?"

And somehow, after that, we just kept talking.

Movies, songs, reels, Ludo, college stories, random updates, calls that somehow become longer conversations than expected... and all the completely unnecessary things that somehow become part of a friendship.

What I genuinely appreciate is how easy it became to talk.

We can be completely stupid one minute, and then somehow end up having a genuinely meaningful conversation the next.

And I think that's one of the things that makes this friendship special.

So, thank you.

For the conversations, the laughs, the random calls, the stories, the recommendations, the chaos, and for simply being someone I can talk to.

I'm genuinely glad that random follow happened.
